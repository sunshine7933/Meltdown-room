Three ways to reconnect

Spiritual ideas can be invitations to notice, imagine, and pause. Take what resonates; leave the rest.

01 /

Third-eye pause

Close your eyes if comfortable. Relax your forehead. Notice one image, feeling, or thought without needing to explain it.

02 /

Set an intention

Choose a word for the next hour—ease, courage, patience, or joy. Let it guide one small action.

03 /

Return to earth

Look around and name three things you can see. Your curiosity and your feet on the ground can coexist.
