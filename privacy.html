The Meltdown Room Privacy Policy

Effective September 2, 2026

Developer: Sunshine
Privacy and support contact: oc2co33610@gmail.com

What the app handles

The Meltdown Room lets you write private reflections, memorial memories, plans, recipes, connection notes, companion preferences, favorites, and other room entries. Most of this content is stored only on your device. If you create an account and choose backup, your account email, settings, and selected room entries are securely stored with our service provider, Supabase, so they can be restored on your devices.

Optional device information

If you allow location access, the app uses it to help find nearby outdoor places. Sunshine does not sell your location. Opening an external map, sharing feature, email, text, telephone, donation, or resource link hands that action to the provider you choose, whose privacy practices then apply. Optional companion photos are described in the app as private to the device and are not uploaded unless the app clearly asks first.

How information is used

Information is used only to provide app features, authenticate accounts, back up and restore selected content, maintain security, and respond to support requests. Sunshine does not sell personal information and does not use it for third-party advertising.

Permissions

The app requests a device permission only when needed for a feature you choose, such as location or selecting a private photo. You can deny or revoke permissions in device settings.

Retention and deletion

Device-only information remains until you remove it in the app, clear the app’s storage, or uninstall the app. Cloud account information remains until you delete your account or it is removed according to operational and legal requirements. Deleting an account deletes its associated cloud data; limited records may be retained only when required for security, fraud prevention, or law.

You can begin account deletion inside Account & Device or at Delete My Account. For privacy questions or deletion help, email oc2co33610@gmail.com.

Children

The app is intended for a general audience and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate parent or guardian involvement.

Changes

Material changes will be posted here with an updated effective date.
